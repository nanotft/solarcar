Structure

The subteams

Electronics

Responsible for all main electronic components — the central control unit, electric motors, solar panels, battery management system, sensors, wiring, and competition repairs.

Software

Writes firmware for microcontrollers, runs aerodynamic simulations, connects sensor data to the central display, handles safety systems, and manages remote communication.

Mechanics

Handles all mechanical and structural components — chassis, brakes, steering, suspension, fluids, canopy, and underside — through CAD design, materials management, and stress testing.

Business

Manages finances, social media, sponsor relations, club communications, website and blog updates, and team apparel and merchandise.
